# Phase 14.1–14.3 — Strategy Edge and Continuous Paper Portfolio

Atlas v4.4.3 adds three connected research capabilities:

1. A deterministic strategy laboratory for parameter sweeps, rejection gates, multi-objective ranking, and champion selection.
2. Market-regime classification and strategy-specific regime gating.
3. A continuous paper-portfolio monitor that scores strategy health and recommends promote, continue, reduce, or pause actions.

All components are research and paper-trading only. They do not submit broker orders.
