# Phase 12.4–12.6: Strategy Performance Intelligence

This release adds three research-only components:

1. `feature_intelligence`: permutation importance, information coefficient, mutual information, redundant-feature detection, stability scoring, and pruning suggestions.
2. `execution_costs`: spread, slippage, square-root market impact, latency, commissions, borrow/financing, participation limits, and default equity/crypto/forex profiles.
3. `strategy_intelligence`: out-of-sample rejection rules, drawdown and cost penalties, composite scoring, grades, ranking, and paper-deployment recommendations.

All outputs are research and paper-trading recommendations. No broker order is submitted.
