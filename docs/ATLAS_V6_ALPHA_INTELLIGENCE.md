# Atlas v6.0 Alpha Intelligence Platform

This release adds a cohesive alpha-discovery subsystem without removing or replacing any
existing Atlas module.

## Capabilities

- Versioned strategy registry and reusable strategy templates
- Deterministic grid and seeded random parameter search
- Multi-dimensional robustness evaluation
- Out-of-sample, walk-forward, Monte Carlo, stability, regime and cost evidence fields
- Experiment fingerprints and in-memory experiment catalog
- Stage-gated promotion from research through production
- Manual approval requirement before promotion beyond small-capital validation
- Integrated discovery workflow that ranks candidates and identifies a qualified champion

## Safety

The subsystem performs research classification only. It does not submit orders, enable broker
credentials, or bypass existing production safety and promotion governance.
